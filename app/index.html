    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions. One playthrough is enough — PowerPyx puts the story at about 60 to 80 hours and the whole run at about 200 hours; Push Square says 250 or more. There are no difficulty settings and no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave).
